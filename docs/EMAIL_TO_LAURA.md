What it is for

The app answers the questions the spreadsheet was meant to answer:

- Is the business making money this month / this year?
- Where is the money coming from, and where is it going?
- Which products are worth selling, once Inkthreadable’s cost is known?
- What did we give away in discounts, refunds and free postage?
- If I change a price or a blank cost, what happens to profit? (This is a what-if only. It does not change the real books.)

There are two halves, and they are not the same number:

1. **Finance** (Cash, Reconciliation, Categories, Data health) — what actually moved in Monzo.
2. **Insights** (Insights, Products, Groups, Customers, Orders, and the rest) — what Shopify sold and what Inkthreadable charged.

A sale is booked on the day the customer orders. The money lands in Monzo days later, after card fees. Cash and Orders will not match pound-for-pound. That is expected. Use **Reconciliation** to join a Shopify day to the Stripe deposit, or an Inkthreadable invoice to the bank charge.

A dash (—) means the app does not have a reliable figure. It will not pretend a missing printer cost is £0.

What you will use most

**Cash** — the P&L from the bank. This is the closest replacement for the old dashboard totals. By default it leaves out salary and tithe; tick **Include salary & tithe** when you want the full net.

**Insights** — a numbered list of findings from figures the app has already calculated. Nothing here is a forecast. The list is rebuilt every time you open the page or change the dates. After you upload a new bank file or update Shopify, come back here and it will follow the new numbers. There is no separate “refresh insights” button.

**Products / Groups / Orders** — units, sales and contribution profit for what sold. Click a product to see that garment, its sizes and colours, and whether it made money.

**Customers, Shipping, Discounts, Refunds** — who is repeat, what postage costs vs what we charged, which codes we used, what came back.

**Simulate** — change a price, a blank cost, postage or a discount and see the effect on the period you picked. Real orders and bank rows are not edited.

**Mapping / Blanks** — which Shopify products are tied to which Inkthreadable garment, so profit can be calculated.

**Categories** — rules that label Monzo rows (Shopify payout, Inkthreadable, software, and so on). You can add or edit a rule here. A category you set by hand is not overwritten.

**Data health** — the only page you need for monthly updates. You do not need a computer terminal.

**i** (top right) — the in-app guide, same content as this overview in more detail.

Every list has **Download this table (CSV)** if you want it in Excel or Numbers.

Each month

1. Export a Monzo CSV and upload it on **Data health → Import bank CSV**. Overlapping months are fine. Rows already held are left alone, including categories you set by hand.
2. Press **Update Shopify**, then **Update Inkthreadable**. Leave the Data health page open. It refreshes itself every 15 seconds until the update finishes.
3. Press **Link supplier orders** so printer jobs attach to Shopify orders.
4. Glance at **Mapping** and **Reconciliation** if anything is still yellow.

Do not upload the Finance Dashboard spreadsheet again unless you are deliberately bringing in older history. The spreadsheet is only read. The app never overwrites it.

The books already include the history we loaded from your existing files and the shop. You do not need to “start again” or run a first-time sync.
